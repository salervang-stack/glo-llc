Billing

  • Monthly plans renew automatically each month until you cancel. Prices are in US dollars.
  • You see the price before you pay. If a price changes, we email you before the new price applies, and you can cancel first.
  • Charges show on your statement as GLO LLC.

Cancelling

  • Cancel any time by emailing us. There is no contract and no cancellation fee.
  • When you cancel, the plan stops renewing and you keep access until the end of the month you already paid for.

Refunds

  • If you were charged by mistake, or the service did not work as described, email us within 14 days of the charge and we refund it.

  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank decides how long it takes to show.
